## Who we are

Jaminly is a free, open-source warranty tracker made by Farid Aiman. In this policy, "Jaminly", "we" and "us" mean the Jaminly project, and "you" means anyone who uses the Jaminly apps or this website.

Questions or requests: [app@jaminly.app](mailto:app@jaminly.app).

## What this policy covers

- The Jaminly apps for iPhone, Android and the web, and the Jaminly service they connect to.
- This website, jaminly.app.

Jaminly is open source, so anyone can run their own copy. If you use a copy run by someone else (a "self-hosted" instance), that person is responsible for your data and this policy does not apply to it.

## Data we collect

### Your account

When you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us. We use them to create your account and to show who is signed in. We check the sign-in token Google gives us and then discard it. We never see your Google password.

### Your warranties

Everything you enter: product name, brand, model, serial number, category, store, purchase date, price, warranty length, what is covered and not covered, notes, and your reminder settings.

### Your receipts

The photos and PDFs you attach as proof of purchase. Receipts can show your address or part of your card number, so we treat them as sensitive (see [how we protect your data](#how-we-protect-your-data)).

### Your devices

If you allow notifications, the app registers a push token: a random identifier that lets us send reminders to that device. We also keep your notification preferences.

To keep you signed in, the app stores a session token on your device. On iPhone and Android it goes in the device's secure storage. On the web it goes in your browser's local storage.

### What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, your contacts, or any photos you haven't chosen to attach. We don't use advertising identifiers. The app has no analytics or tracking. If we ever add usage statistics, they will stay off unless you turn them on.

## How we use your data

| Data | Why we need it | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|
| Account (name, email, photo) | To create your account and sign you in | Until you delete your account |
| Warranties | To show your warranties and work out when they expire | Until you delete them or your account |
| Receipts | So you have proof of purchase when you make a claim | Until you delete them or your account |
| Push token | To send reminders to your device | Until you sign out on that device or delete your account |
| Email address | To send the reminders you turn on, and the code that confirms account deletion | Until you delete your account |

We don't send marketing emails. We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone for their own purposes.

## Who else handles your data

We use a few service providers to run Jaminly. They process your data only on our instructions and only to provide their service to us:

- **Google**, to sign you in.
- **Expo**, to deliver push notifications to your device.
- **An email provider**, to send reminder and confirmation emails. (Provider to be confirmed.)
- **A hosting and storage provider**, to run the Jaminly service and store your data and receipts. (Provider to be confirmed.)

Some of these providers may process data outside Malaysia. When they do, we only use providers that protect it to a comparable standard.

We only give your data to anyone else if the law requires it, and we will tell you when we are allowed to.

## How we protect your data

- All connections use HTTPS.
- Your data and receipts are encrypted at rest.
- Receipts are kept in private storage and are never public. Each time you open one, the app gets a link that expires after a few minutes.
- Only the people who run the service can access its systems, and only when needed to keep it working.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual warranties and receipts in the app at any time.

To delete your whole account, go to **Settings**, then **Delete account** in the app. When you confirm, we delete your account, all your warranties, all your receipts and your push tokens straight away. Copies in our backups are removed within 14 days, as the backups roll over.

Step-by-step instructions, including what to do if you can't use the app, are on the [delete your account](/delete-account) page.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and the EU and UK GDPR, you have the right to:

- see the personal data we hold about you,
- correct it,
- delete it,
- get a copy of it in a portable format,
- object to or limit how we use it, and
- withdraw your consent, for example by turning off notifications.

You can do most of this yourself in the app. For anything else, email [app@jaminly.app](mailto:app@jaminly.app) from your account's email address. We will reply within 21 days.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to your data protection authority. In Malaysia, that is the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

## Children

Jaminly is not meant for children under 13, or under the minimum age for online services where they live. We don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

## This website

jaminly.app is a static website. It has no sign-in and no forms, sets no cookies, and loads no trackers or third-party scripts. It is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, keeps basic request logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for a short time to keep the site secure and running.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we use your data, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
