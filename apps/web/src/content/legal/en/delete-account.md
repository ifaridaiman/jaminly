## Delete your account in the app

This works the same in the iPhone, Android and web apps.

1. Open Jaminly and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings** and choose **Delete account**.
3. Type **delete** to confirm.
4. We email a one-time code to your account's email address. Enter it in the app.
5. Your account is deleted and you are signed out.

Uninstalling the app does **not** delete your account. Use the steps above first.

## What gets deleted

- Your account: name, email address and profile photo.
- All your warranties and everything in them.
- All your receipts, photos and PDFs.
- Your push tokens and notification settings.

This happens straight away and can't be undone. Copies in our backups are removed within 14 days, as the backups roll over.

## What we keep

Nothing linked to your account. Our email provider may keep a delivery record of the confirmation email for a short time, as all email services do.

## If you can't use the app

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, email [app@jaminly.app](mailto:app@jaminly.app) from the email address of the Google account you used with Jaminly, with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm. We will delete your account within 7 days and email you when it's done.

## Delete only some data

You don't have to delete your account to remove data. You can delete any warranty or receipt in the app at any time, and it is removed in the same way.

See the [privacy policy](/privacy) for more about how we handle your data.
